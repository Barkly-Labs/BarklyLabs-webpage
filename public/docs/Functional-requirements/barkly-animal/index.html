Barkly Animal

Human Rehabilitation, Animal Welfare & Protection

1. Mission

Barkly Animal seeks to protect animal welfare while helping people develop healthier, safer, and more compassionate relationships with animals.

We recognize that preventing animal cruelty requires addressing both the needs of animals and the circumstances, behaviors, and challenges of the humans responsible for their care.

2. Core Objectives

Protect animals from cruelty, neglect, exploitation, and preventable suffering.

Help individuals understand the psychological, behavioral, and situational factors contributing to harmful behavior toward animals.

Support accountability, personal growth, and behavioral change.

Provide education on animal behavior, welfare, boundaries, and responsible care.

Help individuals develop the skills necessary for safe and humane animal guardianship.

Support successful, responsible animal ownership when appropriate.

Prioritize the safety and welfare of animals throughout every intervention.

3. Human Support & Rehabilitation

Barkly Animal aims to collaborate with qualified professionals and relevant organizations to provide:

Behavioral education and animal welfare awareness.

Referrals to appropriate mental health and behavioral support.

Compassionate, nonjudgmental opportunities for individuals to discuss harmful behaviors.

Support for developing empathy, emotional regulation, and appropriate coping strategies.

Structured rehabilitation and accountability programs.

Individualized assessments of readiness for animal care.

4. Animal Welfare & Safety

The welfare of animals remains central to all activities.

Barkly Animal will work toward:

Appropriate veterinary assessment and care.

Safe environments for animals.

Individualized animal welfare evaluations.

Humane, evidence-informed handling and care practices.

Safeguarding animals from further harm.

Collaboration with qualified veterinarians, behaviorists, and animal welfare professionals.

Human rehabilitation must never come at the expense of animal safety.

5. Responsible Animal Guardianship

When appropriate, Barkly Animal may help individuals progress toward responsible animal ownership through:

Animal care education and practical training.

Demonstrations of safe, humane handling.

Supervised experiences with animals.

Evaluations of readiness and capability.

Assistance accessing appropriate supplies and veterinary services.

Ongoing support and follow-up.

Animal placement will always prioritize the animal's individual welfare and safety.

6. Guiding Philosophy

Barkly Animal believes that preventing harm requires more than punishment alone.

We seek to understand why harmful behavior occurs, provide opportunities for meaningful change, and protect those who are vulnerable.

We believe people should have opportunities to improve while recognizing that accountability, boundaries, and animal safety are essential.

Compassion for humans and protection for animals are not opposing values. They must work together.
