Barkly Chem

Functional Requirements Specification (FRS)

Parent Organization: Barkly Labs Project: Barkly Chem Version: 1.0 Status: Proposed

1. Purpose

Barkly Chem is a scientific education and harm-reduction initiative within Barkly Labs. Its purpose is to provide accessible, evidence-based, nonjudgmental information about chemistry, pharmacology, psychoactive substances, drug interactions, and substance-related safety.

The initiative will publish educational articles, scientific explainers, and visual informational posters to help individuals make informed decisions about their health and safety.

2. Mission

To make scientific and pharmacological information accessible, understandable, and useful to the public while promoting dignity, informed decision-making, and harm reduction.

3. Functional Requirements

FR-001: Dedicated Website Section

The system SHALL provide a dedicated Barkly Chem section accessible through the existing Barkly Labs website.

FR-002: Independent Branding

The system SHALL support Barkly Chem branding, including its own logo, color palette, typography, and visual identity while retaining attribution to Barkly Labs.

FR-003: Educational Content

The system SHALL support publishing educational materials covering:

Pharmacology and mechanisms of action

Psychoactive substances and their effects

Drug interactions and contraindications

Toxicity, overdose, and adverse effects

Harm reduction and safety education

Chemistry fundamentals and scientific concepts

FR-004: Educational Posters

The system SHALL provide a dedicated gallery for scientific and harm-reduction posters.

Each poster SHALL support:

A title and description

An accessible image display

Publication date

Author or contributor attribution

References and scientific sources

A disclaimer when applicable

FR-005: Individual Content Pages

The system SHALL allow each educational resource to have its own dedicated page and shareable URL.

FR-006: Search and Discovery

The system SHOULD provide search and categorization functionality to help visitors find information by substance, topic, or content type.

FR-007: Scientific References

Educational content SHALL include citations to reputable scientific literature, clinical references, or authoritative public-health sources when factual medical or pharmacological claims are made.

FR-008: Content Review

The system SHOULD support a review workflow before publication to help ensure scientific accuracy, clarity, and safety.

FR-009: Harm-Reduction Standards

Content SHALL be nonjudgmental and SHALL NOT shame, stigmatize, or discriminate against people who use substances.

FR-010: Accessibility

The system SHALL follow WCAG accessibility guidelines, including:

Alternative text for images

Readable typography and contrast

Keyboard navigation

Screen-reader compatibility

Responsive layouts

Accessible descriptions of scientific graphs

FR-011: Responsive Design

The system SHALL support desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

FR-012: Content Management

The system SHOULD support creating, editing, updating, and removing educational materials without requiring extensive manual website development for each publication.

FR-013: Safety Information

Relevant educational pages SHALL include appropriate safety information, warning signs, and guidance on seeking professional or emergency assistance.

FR-014: Educational Disclaimers

The system SHALL clearly distinguish educational information from individualized medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

FR-015: Content Versioning

The system SHOULD maintain publication dates and revision history for scientific resources to support corrections and updates.

4. Nonfunctional Requirements

NFR-001: Performance

Pages SHOULD load efficiently, including on slower internet connections.

NFR-002: Privacy

The system SHALL minimize collection of visitor data and SHALL NOT require accounts to access educational resources.

NFR-003: Security

The system SHALL use HTTPS and follow secure development practices.

NFR-004: Maintainability

The implementation SHOULD use reusable components and a modular architecture consistent with the existing Barkly Labs website.

NFR-005: Reliability

Published educational resources SHOULD remain accessible through stable URLs.

NFR-006: Low Maintenance

The system SHOULD minimize ongoing administrative and technical work for maintainers.

5. Initial Release Scope (MVP)

The first release SHOULD include:

Barkly Chem landing page

Mission and editorial standards

Educational poster gallery

Individual poster pages

Scientific references and disclaimers

Responsive and accessible design

Integration with the existing Barkly Labs navigation and branding

Search, advanced content management, and automated scientific review may be introduced in later releases.

6. Guiding Principle

People are people, not numbers.

Barkly Chem exists to make scientific knowledge more accessible and to support people through education, dignity, and evidence-based harm reduction.
